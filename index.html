Saturday repair clinic

Bring your bicycle on Saturday afternoon and ride home safely the same day.

Bicycle being checked for safe riding at a cycle repair clinic.

When and where

Every Saturday, 15:00 to 17:00, in Workshop B, Student Centre.

How long will my repair take?

Most small repairs take around 15–30 minutes. A standard bicycle check usually takes about 15 minutes, while a full service can take 45–60 minutes.

Please plan your Saturday afternoon accordingly. Repair times may vary depending on the condition of your bike and how busy the clinic is.

Prices

  • A check: EUR 10
  • Each small repair: EUR 5
  • A full service: EUR 45
